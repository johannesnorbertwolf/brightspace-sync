# Brightspace Sync — install guide

Brightspace Sync keeps a copy of all your Brightspace course material on your
Mac and tells you when something new appears. You set it up once; after that it
lives quietly in the menu bar at the top of your screen.

You don't need to know anything technical to use it.

## What you need

- A Mac.
- Google Chrome (free). If you don't have it, get it from
  https://www.google.com/chrome/
- Your RUG Brightspace login, and your phone if you use a two-step check.

## Step 1 — Install Chrome

If Chrome is already on your Mac, skip this. Otherwise open the link above and
install it the normal way.

## Step 2 — Install the app

1. Download **Brightspace Sync** from
   https://github.com/johannesnorbertwolf/brightspace-sync/releases/latest/download/Brightspace-Sync.dmg
2. Open the downloaded disk image, then drag **Brightspace Sync** onto the
   **Applications** shortcut in the same window. Eject the disk image
   afterwards.
3. Open **Applications**, right-click (or Control-click) **Brightspace Sync**,
   choose **Open**, then click **Open** again.

That last extra step is only needed the first time. It appears because the app
is shared directly rather than sold through Apple's App Store.

## Step 3 — Set it up

The setup window opens by itself. Work down the list:

1. **Where to save files.** Keep the suggested folder, or click **Choose** to
   pick another.
2. **Brightspace account.** Click **Sign In**. A Chrome window opens; log in
   as usual, including any two-step check. The window closes on its own when
   you're done.
3. **Courses to sync.** Untick any course you don't want.
4. **Notifications.** Leave "Show macOS notifications" on. If you'd like a
   message on your phone too, tick **iMessage** and enter your number or Apple
   ID email.
5. Click **Start Syncing**.

The first download can take a few minutes. When it finishes, a small icon stays
in the menu bar. That's the app.

## Every day

- The menu bar icon is the app. Click it to see the status, sync right away,
  open your folder, or change settings.
- It checks for new material on its own (every hour by default).
- You'll get a notification when new files, announcements, assignments or
  grades appear.

## Where your files are

- Everything is saved in a **Brightspace** folder in your home folder (or
  wherever you chose).
- Each course has its own folder, with the same module folders as Brightspace.
- Each course also gets simple **Grades**, **Assignments** and
  **Announcements** summaries.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software."**
  Right-click the app and choose **Open** (once). This is normal for apps not
  from the App Store.
- **The menu bar says "Needs sign-in."** Click it, choose **Sign In Again**,
  and log in.
- **It says Chrome is missing.** Install Chrome, then reopen the app.
- **Nothing seems to happen.** Give the first download a few minutes, then
  click the menu bar icon to check the status.

## To remove it

Quit it from the menu bar, drag the app from **Applications** to the Trash, and
delete the **Brightspace** folder if you don't want to keep the files. Your
login is never stored on anyone else's computer.
